## Mission & Background:
- Founded by former architecture students who experienced firsthand the difficulty of sourcing professional drawing tools and verified curriculum materials in Algeria.
- Began as an active Instagram educational hub answering student queries and grew to over 9,000 active students nationwide.
- Today, Trendy Plaza operates both an e-commerce shop delivering physical drafting instruments to all wilayas and a centralized learning system aligned with official academic standards.
- Aligned with recognized architectural curriculum benchmarks and professional software ecosystems (Autodesk AutoCAD/Revit, Trimble SketchUp, EPAU Alger).
